About Us

Crafting excellence in steel since our foundation.

Our Story

Basil Moolman started with a simple vision: to provide the highest quality fabrication and steelwork tailored to the unique needs of our clients. Over the years, we have grown from a small local workshop to a trusted name in the industry.

Our expertise spans across residential upgrades like gates and carports, to heavy-duty industrial structural steel framing. We take pride in our meticulous attention to detail, rigorous safety standards, and our ability to turn client concepts into reality.

Our Mission

To deliver durable, reliable, and aesthetically pleasing steel solutions while maintaining the highest level of customer satisfaction and safety in every project we undertake.

Craftsmanship

Every cut, weld, and polish is executed with precision. We believe in doing things right the first time.

Durability

We use premium materials and proven techniques to ensure our structures stand the test of time and weather.

Integrity

Honest pricing, clear communication, and reliable timelines. We value the trust our clients place in us.
